Everything you need

Built for the job hunt grind

Designed specifically for students applying to dozens of roles at once — without the overwhelm.

🗂️
Kanban Board
Drag and drop cards between Applied, Interviewing, Offer, and Rejected columns — just like a real project board.
⏰
Follow-up Reminders
Set a follow-up date on every application. Overdue reminders are highlighted in red so nothing slips through the cracks.
📊
Live Stats
See your total applications, response rate, and offer count at a glance — always up to date as you move cards.
🔍
Search & Filter
Instantly search by company or role, or filter the board by status. Switch between Kanban and a sortable Table view.
💾
Works Offline
All your data is saved in your browser's local storage. No account, no server, no fees. Your data stays on your device.
📥
Export Your Data
Download all your applications as a JSON file at any time. Your data is always yours to keep, share, or back up.
